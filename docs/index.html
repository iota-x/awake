Safe to walk away.

A closed MacBook in a bag shouldn't stay awake. These rules stop the session for you.

Needs the charger

Sessions need a power adapter by default. One click allows battery when you want it.

Stops at your battery limit

On battery, it ends at the level you choose, or after a time limit you set.

Stops if the Mac runs hot

If macOS reports the Mac is getting hot, the session ends before it becomes a problem.

Choosing Sleep always wins

Pick Sleep from the Apple menu and your Mac sleeps. Awake never argues with you.

Closed-lid mode lasts up to 24 hours, and you get a notification if a rule ends it. If Awake quits or crashes, a small watchdog turns sleep back on. Setting it up asks for your password once, to allow exactly two pmset commands. How closed-lid mode works

Install in a minute.

  1. Download

    Get Awake-1.0.0.dmg. Prefer a zip? It's on the releases page.

  2. Drag to Applications

    Open the disk image and drag Awake into Applications. It lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon.

  3. Open it, once

    Awake isn't signed with a paid Apple developer ID, so the first launch is blocked. Go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. Or run this in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Awake.app
